Frequently asked questions

Is Spider Solitaire free to play?

Yes — completely free, no download, no account, no pop-ups.

What difficulty levels are available?

1 suit (easiest), 2 suits, or 4 suits (hardest). Pick your level before dealing.

Can I undo moves or get a hint?

Yes — use Undo to step back and Hint to highlight a legal move. Full-screen play with keyboard and drag or click.

Does it track my stats?

Your wins, moves and best games are saved in your browser on this device.
